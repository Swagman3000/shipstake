Promises are cheap. Make yours cost something.

Shipstake lets a team lock SOL behind a public promise to ship something by a set date. Ship on time and you get every lamport back, plus a permanent line on your public record. Miss it, and the stake goes to the people who backed you.

§ 02 — Anatomy of a promise

One promise, two possible endings.

Every promise follows the same path through the escrow program. Nobody holds the money in between. The program does, and it can only release it one of two ways.

Lifecycle of a promise
§ 03 — Who decides

Proof first. A reviewer only if it's disputed.

Backers get paid when a team misses, so they can't be the judges. Teams can't grade their own work either. Each promise names a public reviewer wallet before any money is locked.

By the deadline

The team posts proof

It has to meet the test written into the promise. No proof by the deadline is a miss.

Next 72 hours

Backers can challenge

If nobody challenges the proof, the stake goes back to the team.

Within 7 days of a challenge

The reviewer rules

The ruling is final. If the reviewer stays silent, it counts as a miss, because the team chose them.

§ 04 — Track records

A history you can't delete.

Every resolved promise, shipped or missed, stays on the team's public record. See the promise ledger.

§ 05 — Where it stands

On devnet, and unaudited.

The escrow program runs on Solana devnet. It hasn't been audited and isn't on mainnet, so nobody can stake real SOL. Check it yourself.
